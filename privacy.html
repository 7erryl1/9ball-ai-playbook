Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy explains what the 9-Ball AI Playbook app (“the app”), made by Li Hua Ming (“we”), does with information. In short: the app has no accounts and we run no servers, so we do not receive your data. The only information that leaves your iPhone is what Apple needs for purchases and what Google’s ad service collects to show ads.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Layouts and settings – your ball layouts, chosen level, table options and language are saved on your iPhone only.
  • Photos – when you import a layout from a photo, the picture is analysed on your iPhone to find the table and the balls. It is not uploaded, sent to us or kept by the app.
  • Camera – the app asks for camera access only to take the photo of the table when you choose “Take photo”.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored layouts and settings. This does not delete your Apple purchase records or photos you keep in the Photos app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not see your name, Apple Account or payment details; the app only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google’s software in the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks) or other app-specific identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them, and taps in the app such as launches;
  • performance, crash, and other diagnostic information.

This information is collected and used by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Tracking – iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland – you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change your choice from the app.
  • No ads – buying “No ads” or “Pro” stops interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

本政策說明 Li Hua Ming（以下稱「我們」）開發的 9-Ball AI Playbook App（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理資訊。簡單來說：本 App 沒有帳號，我們也沒有架設伺服器，所以不會收到你的資料。離開你 iPhone 的資訊，只有 Apple 處理購買所需的資料，以及 Google 廣告服務為了顯示廣告所收集的資料。

只留在你 iPhone 上的資訊

  • 球型與設定：你擺的球型、程度、球檯選項和語言，只存在你的 iPhone。
  • 照片：用照片匯入球型時，照片在你的 iPhone 上分析球檯和球的位置，不會上傳、不會傳給我們，本 App 也不會保留。
  • 相機：只有在你選擇「拍照匯入」時，本 App 才會要求使用相機拍攝球檯。

刪除本 App 會移除本機儲存的球型與設定，但不會刪除 Apple 的購買紀錄或你保留在「照片」App 中的照片。

購買

App 內購買由 Apple 處理，適用 Apple 隱私權政策。我們看不到你的姓名、Apple 帳號或付款資料；本 App 只會知道你擁有哪些項目，以便解鎖功能。退款由 Apple 處理。

廣告（Google AdMob）

免費版透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。為了顯示及衡量廣告，App 內的 Google 程式可能收集：

  • 裝置的廣告識別碼（只有在 iOS 詢問時你允許追蹤才會使用），或其他 App 專用的識別碼；
  • 由 IP 位址推估的大致位置；
  • 顯示過哪些廣告、你與廣告的互動，以及 App 內的操作（例如開啟 App）；
  • 效能、當機及其他診斷資訊。

這些資訊由 Google 依照「Google 如何使用採用其服務的網站或應用程式所提供的資訊」及 Google 隱私權政策收集和使用。我們不會收到可以識別你身分的資料。

你的選擇

  • 追蹤：iOS 會詢問是否允許本 App 追蹤。選擇「要求 App 不要追蹤」後，廣告不會使用你的廣告識別碼做個人化。你隨時可以在「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」更改。
  • 歐洲經濟區、英國及瑞士：顯示個人化廣告前會先徵求你的同意，你也可以在 App 內更改選擇。
  • 免廣告：購買「免廣告」或「Pro」後不再顯示插頁式廣告。獎勵廣告只有在你主動選擇觀看時才會出現。

兒童

本 App 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們不會刻意收集兒童的資訊。
