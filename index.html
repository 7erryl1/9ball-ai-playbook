Frequently asked questions

How do I get route suggestions?

Tap an object ball: “Safety” appears below the table. Tap a pocket as well: “Attack” appears, with a box for where the cue ball should stop. Drag the box where you want, then tap “Attack”.

How do I import a layout from a photo?

Open the menu (☰) and choose “Take photo” or “From library”. Photograph the whole table from above or from one end, with all four corners in the picture. Make sure the four white points sit on the outer corners of the cushions – the grid lines should run through the diamonds – then tap “Import”. You can tap a ball to correct its number.

I bought something. How do I get it back on a new iPhone?

Menu (☰) → “Upgrade” → “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is finding routes slow?

The app tries thousands of strokes on your phone. It usually takes a second or two; older iPhones may take longer.

常見問題

怎麼取得建議路線？

點一顆子球，球檯下方會出現「防守路線」；再點一個袋口，會出現「進攻路線」和母球停點方框。把方框拖到想要的位置，再按「進攻路線」。

怎麼用照片匯入球型？

打開選單（☰），選「拍照匯入」或「從相簿匯入」。從上方或球檯一端拍下整張球檯，四個角都要入鏡。確認四個白點在庫邊外緣的四個角（格線會穿過顆星），再按「匯入球型」。號碼不對可以點球修改。

換了新 iPhone，買過的功能怎麼恢復？

選單（☰）→「升級與購買」→「還原購買」，並使用購買時的同一個 Apple 帳號。

怎麼申請退款？

購買由 Apple 處理，請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

找路線為什麼要等？

App 會在手機上試打幾千桿來找路線，通常一兩秒；較舊的 iPhone 可能久一點。
